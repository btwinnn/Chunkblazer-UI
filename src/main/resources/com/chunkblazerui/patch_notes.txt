## Chunk paths and quest calculator
- Right-click the world map (or Shift + right-click a tile) and pick Path here, or Alt + click the world map (Alt + click again clears it): the path only goes through chunks you've unlocked
- Stairs, shortcuts, boats, gliders and the like are used when both ends are in your chunks
- Quests have a Calc button that works out the chunks they still need, plus the ones to walk there
