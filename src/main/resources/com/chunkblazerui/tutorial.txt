# --- Tutorial Island ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Gielinor Guide's house, where a new account starts: x1,y1,x2,y2 in world tiles. It's

# A square round Lumbridge's spawn, kept clear (and fenced) while a new account is set up

3218,3214,3226,3222

# The very first box, on either start: Task Sound Volume, with a Test.
[sound|Confirm]
Sound check
How loud ChunkBlazer's card and task sounds are. Press Test, then go up a step at a time.

[welcome|Next]
Welcome to ChunkBlazer
The world is split into chunks, and you can only use the ones you've unlocked. Everything is locked for now.

[itaskshow|Confirm]
Where's your task?
Pick where your tracked task shows: the side panel, a box on screen (the widget), or both. You can change it later in the task list's Settings.

[iassist|Confirm]
How much help?
Pick your assists: outlines on what tasks need, task details, menus and tooltips. Change any of them later in Settings > Assists.

[iunlock|Unlock this chunk]
Your first chunk
Unlock the chunk you're standing in. Chunks are free on Tutorial Island.

[imap]
Your first chunk
Open the world map (by your minimap) to see it.

[map]
The world map
Your chunks are in colour, locked ones are dark. Close the map to carry on.

[walls|Next]
The walls
The glowing walls are the edge of your chunks. On the island, walking into a new chunk unlocks it for free.

[tasks|Deal my tasks]
Tasks and points
Each chunk has tasks. Finish tasks for points, spend points on chunks.

These practice tasks are worth 0 points.

[deal]
Your practice tasks
Click a card to flip it, then again to put it away.

[iorb]
Your task list
Click the fire orb by your minimap.

[itrack]
Track a task
Click a task to track it.

[iclose]
Close the task list
Press Esc or click the cross.

[practice]
Practice tasks
Do these as you go through the island.

[newchunk|Got it]
A new chunk
You walked into a locked chunk. On the island it unlocks for free; on the mainland you'd need points.

[themes|Confirm]
Pick a look
Click a card to try a theme, then Confirm. You can change it later in the task list's Settings.

[tokens|Got it]
Boss Tokens
Boss chunks cost a Boss Token, not points. You start with 2, and each boss's first kill gives you another.

[mode]
Choose how you'll play
This is permanent for this account.

[casual]
Casual
Any account, any start. Casual leaderboard.

[competitive]
Competitive
New ironmen only (Group too). Main leaderboard. Needs Server Sync.

[leave]
On to the mainland
Practice done. Finish the island as normal; you'll pick a mode on the mainland.

[arrive|Choose]
Welcome to the mainland
Practice is over: from here it counts. Set up Server Sync and your mode, then unlock Lumbridge.

[sync]
Server Sync
Saves your progress online for leaderboards and other computers. Competitive needs it. You can change it later in the side panel.

[node|Got it]
Group ironman
Everyone in your group plays their own ChunkBlazer. Chunks, tasks and points aren't shared.

# --- Starting from Lumbridge (the plugin turned on outside Tutorial Island) --------------

[lwelcome|Next]
Welcome to ChunkBlazer
The world is split into chunks, and you can only use the ones you've unlocked. Everything is locked until your account is set up.

[lunlock|Next]
Unlocking chunks
Spend points to unlock chunks next to ones you own. Each costs a bit more than the last.

[ltasks|Next]
Tasks and points
Each chunk has tasks. Finish tasks for points, spend points on chunks.

[ltokens|Next]
Boss Tokens
Boss chunks cost a Boss Token, not points. You start with 2, and each boss's first kill gives you another.

[group|Next]
Group ironman
Everyone in your group plays their own ChunkBlazer. Chunks, tasks and points aren't shared.

[home|Unlock Lumbridge]
Your first chunk
All set. Unlock Lumbridge (and your current chunk on Casual) to get your first tasks.

[verify|Choose again]
Setting up your account
Casual: just a moment.

Competitive: when a code shows in chat, type it in public chat.

# --- The game's interface, once a mode is picked ------------------------------------------

[cards]
Your first tasks
Click a card to flip it, then again to put it away.

[orb]
Your task list
Click the fire orb by your minimap.

[track]
Track a task
Click a task to track it.

[close]
Close the task list
Press Esc or click the cross.

[taskshow|Confirm]
Where's your task?
Pick where your tracked task shows: the side panel, a box on screen (the widget), or both. You can change it later in Settings.

[assist|Confirm]
How much help?
Pick your assists: outlines on what tasks need, task details, menus and tooltips. Change any of them later in Settings > Assists.

[tracker|Next]
The task box
Your tracked task shows here with its progress. Hover it for details.

[boxpath]
Show or hide the path
The path button next to the X hides the line to your task, in game and on the maps, and shows it again. Click it twice: off, then back on.

[boxmode]
Bigger or smaller
Click the little button at the top of the task box to switch it between detailed and compact. On the compact one, hover it first.

[boxbook]
Put a task aside
Click the book on the task box to archive this task: it leaves your list until you bring it back. Don't worry, you'll bring it back next.

[archived]
Archived tasks
Archived tasks don't show in your list, outlines or menus. Open the task list with the fire orb, then click Archived.

[unarchive]
Bring it back
Click the book beside the task you just archived to bring it back.

[newtab]
The New tab
Click New: tasks you've just become able to do and your newest chunk's tasks show here. The fire orb glows when there's something new.

[filters]
Search, filters and sort
Search by name, filter by skill, tier or type, and change the order: click Filters to have a look. This chunk only, at the bottom, shows tasks where you're standing.

[settings]
Settings
Click the cog: your look, task box and assists like outlines and the Tasks menu. You can restart this tutorial there too.

[unlocking|Next]
Unlocking chunks
With enough points, walk through a glowing wall to unlock the chunk past it.

[save]
Save a task
Click a task's star (or right-click it) to save it.

[sclose]
Close the task list
Press Esc or click the cross.

[boss]
Saved tasks
Click the saved tasks button to see them. The dragon orb counts your Boss Tokens.

[strack]
Track a saved task
Click a task in your saved tasks to track it.

[panel|Next]
The side panel
The fire icon on RuneLite's sidebar has your tasks, search and filters, and your settings.

[done|Finish]
You're ready
Do tasks, earn points, unlock chunks. Good luck!
